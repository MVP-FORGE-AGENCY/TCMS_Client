# Training Session Procedures

This document outlines the standard operating procedures for conducting training sessions in compliance with EASA Part-ORA and ADR.OR.D.017 requirements.

## 1. Session Scheduling

### 1.1 Planning Requirements

- Training sessions must be scheduled at least **7 days** in advance
- Sessions must have an assigned **authorized instructor**
- Maximum capacity is determined by the training programme configuration
- Location and resources must be confirmed before enrollment opens

### 1.2 Instructor Authorization

Instructors must meet the following criteria:

- Valid instructor qualification for the specific programme
- Current competence (no expired mandatory training)
- No conflict of interest with trainees
- Registered in the system with instructor privileges

## 2. Participant Enrollment

### 2.1 Enrollment Process

1. Training Manager or authorized staff selects participants
2. System validates:
   - Participant is active in the organization
   - No blocking conditions (remedial required, waiting period, max attempts)
   - Absence requirements are satisfied (refresher/initial as needed)
3. Participants receive notification of enrollment

### 2.2 Enrollment Blocking Conditions

Enrollment may be blocked if:

- **Remedial training required** - Previous failure requires remedial before resit
- **Waiting period active** - Minimum days between attempts not elapsed
- **Maximum attempts reached** - Escalation to Training Manager required
- **Absence flag active** - Must complete refresher or initial training first

> **Admin Override**: Training Managers can override blocking conditions with documented justification.

## 3. Attendance Requirements

### 3.1 Recording Attendance

For each session, attendance must be recorded as:

- **Present** - Trainee participated fully
- **Partial** - Trainee attended but participation was incomplete
- **Absent** - Trainee did not attend
- **Excused** - Absence pre-approved with valid reason

### 3.2 Minimum Attendance

- Trainees must be marked **Present** to receive a result
- **Partial** attendance requires Training Manager review
- **Absent** trainees must be rescheduled

## 4. Recording Results

### 4.1 Theory Assessment

- Results recorded as numerical score (0-100%)
- Pass threshold configured per programme (typically 75%)
- Assessment method: Written, Oral, or Computer-Based

### 4.2 Practical Assessment

- Results recorded as numerical score (0-100%)
- Pass threshold configured per programme (typically 70%)
- Assessment method: Practical demonstration, Simulation, or Observation

### 4.3 Overall Result Logic

| Theory | Practical | Overall  |
| ------ | --------- | -------- |
| Pass   | Pass      | **Pass** |
| Pass   | Fail      | **Fail** |
| Fail   | Pass      | **Fail** |
| Fail   | Fail      | **Fail** |

## 5. Instructor Signature

### 5.1 Electronic Signature Requirements

- Instructor must sign session upon completion
- Signature timestamp is recorded
- Session cannot be modified after signing (locked)

### 5.2 Signature Process

1. Instructor reviews all recorded results
2. Confirms attendance accuracy
3. Submits electronic signature
4. Session status changes to **Completed**

## 6. Documentation & Records

### 6.1 Required Records

- Session details (date, location, programme)
- Instructor identity
- Participant list with attendance
- Individual results with scores
- Instructor signature with timestamp

### 6.2 Retention Period

All training records are retained for a minimum of **5 years** as per regulatory requirements.
